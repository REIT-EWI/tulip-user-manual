# Limitations & Feedback

## Current Limitations (Pilot Phase)

- **OpenAI feature parity:** Some OpenAI features (e.g., images, audio, realtime APIs) are not available
- **Model stability:** Model aliases are stable, but the backing models may change as the platform evolves. See [Available Models](./model-mapping.md) for the current mapping
- **Service stability:** The service is under active development. Expect occasional interruptions or updates

## Reporting Issues & Feedback

Your feedback directly shapes the next iteration of TULIP. Please report:

- **Inconsistent or incorrect model behavior**
- **Reliability issues or latency spikes**
- **Suggestions for new models or capabilities**

**How to report:**

- **Technical issues** (unexpected behavior, errors, latency): [open a GitLab issue](https://gitlab.ewi.tudelft.nl/tulip/user-manual/-/issues)
- **Other feedback or questions:** Email [tulip-reit@tudelft.nl](mailto:tulip-reit@tudelft.nl)
